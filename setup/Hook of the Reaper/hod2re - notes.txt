HOD2RE Recoil - ~40ms on, good for Recoil_Value



If you want to put light gun into 4:3 mode, and supported by the light gun. Make the changes below to the DefaultLG game file.

Change:
[States]
:mame_start
*All
>Open_COM
:mame_stop
*All
>Close_COM

To:
[States]
:mame_start
*All
>Open_COM
>AspectRatio_4:3
:mame_stop
*All
>AspectRatio_16:9
>Close_COM


2nd Option, if Light Gun Needs to be out of Feedback Mode to Change Aspect Ratio:
[States]
:mame_start
*All
>Open_COM_NoInit
>AspectRatio_4:3
>Open_COM
:mame_stop
*All
>Close_COM_InitOnly
>AspectRatio_16:9
>Close_COM_NoInit
